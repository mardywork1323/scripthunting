Questions

What counts as a prompt?

Each message you send to SH.AI, on the website or in the SH.AI Agent, is one prompt, including follow-ups like “improve it”. If the AI fails to answer, that try doesn't count.

Do I need an account to use the snippets?

No. The snippet library, search and copy buttons are free for everyone, with no account.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.

Which languages can the AI write?

C, C++, C#, JavaScript and Python, plus game engines and mod tools such as Unity, Godot, Roblox Luau and Java Minecraft mods.
