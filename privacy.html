Privacy Policy

This page explains what information ScriptHunting uses and why. We collect as little as we can.

What we collect

  • If you only search snippets: nothing personal. Your browser remembers small preferences, like your last chosen language, on your own device.
  • If you use the AI helper as a guest: a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, used only to count your daily prompts. It is deleted after two days.
  • If you sign in: an account ID, your name and your email address from the sign-in option you chose (Google, Apple or Microsoft), handled through Google Firebase Authentication. If you use Apple and choose to hide your email, we only get a private relay address. We also keep your daily prompt count and your plan.
  • If you buy a plan: Lemon Squeezy handles your payment. We receive your email, plan and subscription status. We never see or store your card details.
  • Your AI conversations: your messages are sent to the AI to write your answer. Your chat history is kept in your own browser so you can continue later. We don't store your conversations on our server.

Who we share it with

  • Anthropic (Claude API) processes your AI messages to write replies.
  • Google Firebase handles sign-in, together with Google, Apple or Microsoft depending on which you choose.
  • Cloudflare runs our server and stores prompt counts and plans.
  • Lemon Squeezy processes payments.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.

We don't sell your information and we don't show ads.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time, and clear your chat history with "New chat" or by clearing your browser data. To have your account information deleted, use the contact details on this page (being added soon).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
