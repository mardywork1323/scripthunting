Refund Policy

We want you to be happy with your plan. Here's how refunds and cancellations work.

Cancelling

You can cancel any time from the link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for, and you won't be charged again.

Refunds

  • First payment: if you're not happy, ask for a refund within 7 days of your first payment and you'll get your money back in full.
  • Renewals: monthly renewals aren't refunded, but you can cancel before the next one. Yearly renewals can be refunded within 7 days of the renewal date.
  • Problems on our side: if the AI helper was unavailable for a long time, ask us and we'll make it right.

Refunds go back to your original payment method through Lemon Squeezy and can take a few days to appear.

How to ask

To ask for a refund, use the contact details on this page (being added soon) with the email address you paid with.
